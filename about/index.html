About

Engineers first. Consultants second.

Algopop is an independent software studio. We started it because we kept watching good ideas get lost between sales teams, slide decks, and handoffs to whoever was on the bench. So we built the opposite.

Our story

Small on purpose.

We are a deliberately small team of senior engineers. We take on a handful of clients at a time so each one gets our full attention, and the people you meet on the first call are the people who do the work.

Our work spans 6 practices, from real-time voice AI to ERP migrations, because real businesses don’t have problems that fit neatly into one box. What ties it together is a bias for shipping: working software in production, documented well enough that you never depend on us to keep it running.

Principles

What we hold to.

  • 01

    Senior people only

    The engineers you meet on the first call are the engineers who write your code. Nothing gets passed down to a junior bench.

  • 02

    You own everything

    Code, infrastructure, accounts, and documentation belong to you from the first commit. No lock-in, no hostage situations.

  • 03

    Boring where it counts

    Proven tools for the foundation and new technology only where it earns its keep. Your software should outlive the hype cycle.

  • 04

    Straight answers

    We tell you what something will cost, what could go wrong, and when you don’t need us. Trust is the whole business.
